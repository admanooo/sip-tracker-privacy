Privacy Policy

SIP Tracker · Last updated 23 September 2026

SIP Tracker is a personal finance app that helps you track systematic investment plans (SIPs) in Indian mutual funds, watch NSE/BSE and US stocks or ETFs, get price move alerts, and optionally run hosted stock forecasts. Holdings stay on your device.

Data we collect

We do not create user accounts and we do not operate a backend that stores your portfolio. The app stores on the phone:

  • SIP and stock holdings you enter (nickname, fund or ticker, units, amounts, alert percent, alert floors)
  • How many of the free forecasts you have used
  • A hashed PIN and optional biometric unlock preference
  • Cached prices and up to two years of daily history for funds you added

This data is not backed up to Google cloud backup. Screenshots of the app itself are blocked in the Play release.

Photo import

If you use Import from photo, the app reads text from a picture you take or a screenshot you choose. That happens on the device with on-device text recognition. The image is not uploaded to us. We do not operate a server that stores your statement photos. You confirm each fund before it is added to the tracker.

Network requests

When you search funds or refresh prices, the app requests public market data from:

  • mfapi.in — Indian mutual fund NAVs
  • Yahoo Finance — NSE/BSE and US stock, fund, and ETF prices and ticker search

Those services may see your IP address and the fund identifiers you look up. We do not send your PIN, holdings amounts, or identity to them beyond what is needed to fetch a price (scheme code or ticker).

Stock Forecast (free trial, then paid)

SIP tracking, stock prices, and alerts are free. The first 20 hosted stock forecasts are free; after that, unlimited forecasts are an optional Google Play purchase (one-time product forecast_unlock and/or subscription forecast_monthly). Payment is processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card details, name, or email.

Each time you tap Get forecast (free or paid), the app sends the ticker symbol and forecast horizon to the hosted forecast service. It does not send your PIN, units, invested amounts, or other holdings. Forecast output is an illustration, not investment advice.

Notifications

If you enable alerts, the app checks prices in the background about once an hour and notifies you on this device when a tracked SIP or stock rises or falls by the percent you set versus the previous close, or drops below a floor you set. Alerts are worked out on the phone; nothing about them is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Remove a holding inside the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it to erase everything. There is no account and no server copy to delete. Cancel a subscription any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch public prices and forecasts
  • Google Play Billing — optional forecast purchase
  • Run at startup — to keep background price checks scheduled after a reboot
  • Notifications — optional price alerts
  • Biometric / fingerprint — optional unlock
  • Camera — optional, only if you take a photo of a holdings screen to import SIPs

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

Prices come from public data sources and may be delayed or incomplete. SIP Tracker is not a broker, not investment advice, and not a substitute for your fund house or brokerage statements.
